# Salon Management System

A complete Salon Management System built to manage salon operations including appointments, services, products, staff attendance, waiting lists, billing, and multi-branch management.

---

## Features

### Multi Branch Management
- Manage multiple salon branches
- Separate staff and inventory for each branch
- Branch-wise reports and analytics

### Service Management
- Add/Edit/Delete services
- Service categories
- Pricing and duration management
- Staff-wise service assignment

### Product Management
- Manage salon products
- Inventory and stock tracking
- Low stock alerts
- Product sales management

### Appointment Management
- Online & offline appointment booking
- Calendar scheduling
- Appointment status tracking
- Staff assignment

### Waiting List Management
- Add walk-in customers to waiting queue
- Auto assign available staff
- Real-time waiting updates

### Staff Management
- Add and manage staff
- Staff roles and permissions
- Schedule management

### Staff Attendance
- Daily attendance tracking
- Check-In / Check-Out
- Attendance reports
- Leave management

### Customer Management
- Customer profiles
- Service history
- Loyalty points & memberships

### Billing & Payments
- Invoice generation
- Multiple payment methods
- Discounts and tax management

### Reports & Analytics
- Sales reports
- Appointment reports
- Staff performance reports
- Inventory reports

## User Roles

### Admin
- Full system access
- Manage branches, staff, reports, products, and services

### Branch Manager
- Manage branch operations
- Staff and appointments handling

### Receptionist
- Handle customer bookings
- Manage waiting lists and billing

### Staff
- View appointments
- Mark attendance
- Update service status
